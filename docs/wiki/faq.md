# FAQ

## Does raul change my game folder?

No. raul keeps your mods, profiles and downloads in its own storage folder.

## Do I need to own the game?

Yes. You need your own copy of Fallout: New Vegas, and of Fallout 3 if you want Tale of Two Wastelands. raul ships and
downloads no game files.

## Which systems does it run on?

Linux, Windows and macOS. Windows and macOS still need a full test on real computers.

## My computer warns me about the download.

The packages are not signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS may warn about an unknown maker. Check the download
against `SHA256SUMS` as described in [Getting started](getting-started.md). Signing is planned.

## Is Tale of Two Wastelands required?

No. It is optional, and you can add it later.

## What is LOD?

The land, objects and trees you see far away. With mods, the game's own far view no longer matches the world, so
raul builds a new one for your mods. If you change your mods, build it again.

## What is the demo? Is it the real app?

No. The [demo](https://jorisjonkers-dev.github.io/rauls-trusty-toolbox/demo/) is the design of the next window, with made-up data. It does not touch a game. The
[guided tour](https://jorisjonkers-dev.github.io/rauls-trusty-toolbox/tour/) walks you through it.

## Where are my demo choices stored?

In your browser only. The tour has a Reset button.

## Can I use raul from a terminal?

Yes. Every page has a command; see [Using raul from the command line](../USAGE.md).

## Can I contribute?

See [Contributing](contributing.md).

## Where do I report a problem?

Open an [issue](https://github.com/JorisJonkers-dev/rauls-trusty-toolbox/issues). Do not attach game files or mod
archives.
