Vanilla against Beautiful

Left: the vanilla game. No mods, the game's own renderer (the original preset), official files only.

Right: Beautiful. The bundled Beautiful profile (lists/beautiful.toml), with all of raul's engine features (the modern preset: ambient occlusion, sun shadows, reflections, fill light) and a generated far view. Engine nv-rs e4255c837.

The Capital Wasteland views come from Tale of Two Wastelands. Left there is Tale of Two Wastelands alone, with no other mods and the original preset; right is Beautiful on top of it. Tale of Two Wastelands gives the Capital its own sepia sky, in both pictures.

Drag the handle, or focus a slider and use the arrow keys (Home and End jump to the ends). Screenshots only; no game files are published.
